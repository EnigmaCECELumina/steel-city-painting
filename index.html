What Brent does

Useful work for real homes.

Not every project needs a big crew. These are the painting, repair, and finishing jobs that benefit from experience and a steady hand.

Interior & exterior painting

Walls, ceilings, doors, trim, siding, and cabinets—prepared properly and finished with care.

  • Colour changes and repainting
  • Trim, doors, and baseboards
  • Exterior touch-ups and staining

Drywall & plaster repair

Repairing holes, dents, cracks, water damage patches, seams, and tired surfaces before paint goes on.

  • Patching and feathering
  • Minor plaster and drywall work
  • Sanding and spot priming

General handyman work

Practical repairs and maintenance that help keep a home safe, functional, and looking its best.

  • Fixtures and hardware
  • Small carpentry and trim
  • Doors, caulking, and punch lists

About Brent

A small business with plenty of experience.

Steel City Painting & Handyman Services is built around a practical approach: listen carefully, explain the work, and take the time to leave it right.

After years of working in homes, Brent understands that a contractor’s job is about more than paint on a wall. It is about showing up when promised, protecting the space, communicating when something changes, and treating people kindly.

Experience you can see

Brent has been painting and maintaining homes for more than 15 years. He knows where shortcuts show and where preparation matters.
